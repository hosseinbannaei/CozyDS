🎮 CozyDS Prototype

A touch-first, portrait Android layout concept for Pokémon SoulSilver and dual-screen Nintendo DS games.

📱 The Slide-Up "Poké-Drawer"

The DS Top Screen renders in high-res edge-to-edge. The Bottom Screen (Pokégear / Bag) lives in a fluid drawer that slides up on tap or swipe!

⚡ Dynamic Tactile Controls

Virtual D-pad & buttons automatically tuck away or dim when the drawer opens, giving 100% focus to touch interactions.

✨ High-Res 3D Upscaling

The melonDS core renders 3D models at 4x native resolution, making Pokémon and buildings pin-sharp on modern phone screens.
